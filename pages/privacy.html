Privacy Policy.

1. Overview

Silangan is a static school project website about Filipino food and culture. This policy covers the current version of the Site. Account creation, social sign-in, and location search are unavailable.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

The Near Me page shows Albay as a sample area; it does not request your location or search for nearby places. If you contact us or visit the Site, these data practices apply:

  • Email messages: if you email us, we receive the message and the email address used to send it.
  • Hosting records: the hosting provider may process technical information to deliver the Site. We have not verified what it records or how long it keeps those records.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use email messages you send us to respond to your questions or requests. The current version does not use account or location-search information because those features are unavailable.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

The current Site code does not use cookies or browser local storage for sign-in or preferences. We have not verified whether the hosting provider uses cookies or stores technical data when delivering the Site.

5. Sharing Your Information

The current Site does not connect to Google or Facebook sign-in, and it does not perform location searches. The hosting provider delivers the Site, but we have not verified what technical records it may process or retain. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the Site.

6. Third-Party Services

Google and Facebook sign-in are unavailable on this static version of the Site. The Site may link to other websites. If you follow a link, that website handles information you provide under its own privacy policy. Review its policy before sharing information.

7. Security and Retention

The current Site does not keep account profiles or location-search histories because those features are unavailable. We have not verified the hosting provider's security practices or how long it keeps technical records. We have also not verified how long email services retain messages you send us. Because this is a school project, the Site may change or be removed when the project ends.

8. Your Choices and Rights

To ask about information you have sent by email, please see the contact details below. The current static Site does not create accounts or save location-search records for us to retrieve, correct, or delete. We have not verified what technical records the hosting provider holds.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local laws, such as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) in the Philippines.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site is not intended for children under 13. It does not offer account creation or location search. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If you keep using the Site after changes take effect, you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please email us at davidgregorio.dev@gmail.com.
